# Predictive Maintenance

Predictive maintenance uses changes in vibration, temperature, current, and
acoustic signals to find faults before a machine stops working. For the motor
project, bearing faults often appear as changes in vibration amplitude and
frequency content.

Useful signals:

- vibration spectrum from an [[Concepts/IMU Sensors|IMU]];
- motor current under a stable load;
- trend comparison against a normal baseline.

See [[Projects/Motor Anomaly Detection]] for the practical prototype.
